### Baja Divide important files

These are the most current gpx and route guide files for the Baja Divide Bikepacking route.  This site is hosted by Pat Boyle, one of the admins for the Baja Divide Facebook group.  This is where the most recent versions of the route guide and the gpx file of the route are hosted.  The gpx can be loaded into your navigation device of choice.

FAQ:
How do I report issues with this site or the files in it?  Contact Pat Boyle via the Baja Divide Facebook group.

Why do you use Caltopo?  It's free and easy to edit.

Why aren't these files hosted on the original Baja Divide website?  We (the Facebook group admins) don't have access to the original Wordpress site so we host the files here.  GitHub.io is free hosting for us. By hosting them here they are available even to people who don't use Facebook (though the FB group is useful and recommended).

How do I load these files into my gps device?  Each device is different. You may have problems with the route having too many points to load into older devices.  There are tools online to cut GPX files.  Modern devices will load the files without problems.

What apps should I use for navigation?  I recommend GaiaGPS and Caltopo.  I recommend having two devices, one for the route on your bike and your phone as backup and for a good quality map.  Use apps that work well in airplane mode so you conserve battery.  I personally use an iPhone with GaiaGPS and an old Garmin etrex 20x on my bike which uses AA batteries, but that's a bit out of date. Garmin makes more modern devices as do many other manufacturers.

Why don't you upload these routes to other apps like RidewithGPS, OnX and Komoot?  We probably should, but then it's a bit harder for us to maintain so we provide all the data here.

How do you know what to update?  It's been a few years since I rode the route (2 different trips), but by reading the FB posts and some communication with Salvador at FASS bikes we make relevant or important changes.
